# Quorum demo: narration (read this out, in order)

**How to record it:** read the 18 numbered parts below, in order, in one continuous take. Leave about one second of silence between parts.
Don't read the numbers or the titles out loud. Only read the text under each one. About 2 minutes 50 seconds in total.
Save it as `voiceover.mp3` (or `.wav` / `.m4a`) and put it in this folder: `video/`.

---

**1 · The problem**
Every team has the same fight. Three weeks after a meeting, someone asks: wait, didn't we decide the opposite? Nobody remembers, and the work was already built on the old decision.

**2 · Speak it**
This is Quorum. You don't fill in forms. You hold Wispr Flow and just talk, the way you'd tell a colleague what happened.

**3 · Agents**
Five agents clean up the ramble, pull out the decisions, tasks and owners, and compare every new decision with everything your team has agreed before.

**4 · The conflict**
Here's the catch. Last week we said launch on the twentieth. Today I said the twenty-eighth. Quorum shows both side by side, with the exact words, and I decide: keep the old one, or accept the new one.

**5 · Ripple**
And this is what no note-taker does. The landing page was planned around the old date, so Quorum moves it eight days, and drafts a heads-up for its owner. I can switch any change off.

**6 · Tasks and cleanup**
Quorum also pulled out the task, with an owner, and a date it worked out from the word Monday. Your original words are always kept, with fillers struck out and dates fixed.

**7 · Spoken edit**
Something wrong? Just say it. Move the beta testers message to Tuesday. Quorum edits the review and shows exactly what it changed.

**8 · Approve**
Nothing is saved or sent until I approve. One click: the task is created, the old decision is retired, and the team gets their heads-up.

**9 · Board**
Approved tasks land on a team board, one column per person. With Notion or GitHub connected, each task is created there too, with a link back.

**10 · Reminders**
Reminders follow the demo clock. Jump to Tuesday morning, and everyone with something due that day gets a nudge, on Telegram when it's connected.

**11 · Digest**
Every person gets a weekly digest, written for them: what's done, what's due, and what needs a nudge.

**12 · Memory**
The memory keeps every decision and its history, so you can see exactly when, and why, the team changed its mind.

**13 · Time machine**
Pick any date, and see what the team believed that day.

**14 · Brief and radar**
Before the next meeting, a sixty-second brief. And a radar for the decisions that keep changing.

**15 · Ask**
Ask your meetings anything. You get the answer, with the exact words from the meeting.

**16 · Warnings**
Quorum also watches for trouble. Three tasks for one person on the same day? A task due after the launch? It warns you before you approve.

**17 · Upload**
Prefer to bring a transcript or a recording? Drop in a text, V-T-T or S-R-T file, or audio.

**18 · Outro**
Quorum. Meetings that remember. Built entirely by voice with Wispr Flow.
